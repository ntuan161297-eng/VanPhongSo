================================================================================
          HUONG DAN CAI DAT VA VAN HANH MAY CHU VAN PHONG SO (24/24)
================================================================================

Bo cai dat nay da duoc dong goi tron ven (All-In-One):
- Khong can phai cai dat Node.js hay bat ky phan mem nao them tren may chu!
- Chay duoc tren moi may Windows (Windows 10, Windows 11, Windows Server).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BUOC 1: COPY THU MUC NAY SANG MAY CHU 24/24
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Chep toan bo thu muc "Goi_Cai_Dat_May_Chu_Van_Phong_So" vao o C:\ hoac D:\ cua may chu 24/24
  (Vi du de tai: C:\MayChuVanPhongSo hoac Desktop cua may do).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BUOC 2: KHOI DONG THU MAY CHU
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Nhao dup chuot vao file: [ Khoi-Dong-May-Chu.bat ]
- Cua so hien len se thong bao:
  + "Tren may chu nay, mo trinh duyet vao: http://localhost:5173"
  + "Tren CAC MAY KHAC trong phong: http://192.168.x.x:5173"
- Hay ghi lai dia chi IP do (vi du: 192.168.1.50:5173) de gui cho moi nguoi trong phong truy cap!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BUOC 3: CAI DAT TU DONG CHAY KHI MAY TINH BAT LEN (RAT QUAN TRONG CHO MAY 24/24)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Nhao dup chuot vao file: [ Cai-Tu-Dong-Bat-Cung-Windows.bat ]
- Xong! Tu gio, moi khi may tinh duoc bat hoac khoi dong lai sau khi cup dien,
  may chu se tu dong bat len ma khong can thao tac thu cong.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LUU Y VE TUONG LUA (WINDOWS FIREWALL):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Neu tren may chu vao duoc (http://localhost:5173) nhung cac may khac trong phong
  khong vao duoc, hay lam nhu sau tren may chu:
  1. Mo Start menu -> Tim kiem "Windows Defender Firewall"
  2. Chon "Allow an app or feature through Windows Defender Firewall"
  3. Hoac mo cong (Port) 5173 TCP trong Inbound Rules.
